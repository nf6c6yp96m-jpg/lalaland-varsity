How it works

League rules in short

Keepers

  • Keep up to 8 players each fall. No resets, no max years.
  • Keeper price is last season's salary + $5.
  • Waiver pickups: salary = your FAB bid ($1 minimum).
  • Traded players keep their salary.
  • Cut a player and re-add him the same season, and he keeps his original salary.
Next auction budget = $200 − keeper salaries ± traded cap $ + consolation prize
